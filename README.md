**Performance and cryptography.** First author of *An Architectural Performance Evaluation of Post-Quantum and Classical Digital Signature Algorithms on x86 and ARM*, under review at SSCAD 2026. I benchmarked ML-DSA, SLH-DSA and Falcon against RSA and ECDSA on two architectures and decomposed the ARM/x86 gap into clock, vectorized-backend coverage, and a microarchitectural residual. The finding is that whether a library ships a vectorized backend for the target ISA predicts the gap better than clock speed, SIMD width, or cryptographic ISA extensions do.
